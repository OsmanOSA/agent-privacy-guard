Public name lexicon sources (prepared locally; raw and compiled data are not
included in the Git repository).

Producer: Institut national de la statistique et des études économiques (INSEE).

1. Fichier des prénoms, published 16 July 2026.
   Births in France, 1900-2025; list of given names attributed at least 3 times.
   https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8595130

2. Fichier des noms, published 22 May 2018.
   Births in France, 1891-2000; national surnames attributed at least 30 times.
   https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/3536630

The project's MIT license applies to its code, not to these source data.
The INSEE general data reuse terms allow commercial reuse unless stated
otherwise, subject to preserving information integrity and precise source
attribution. See the official terms:
https://www.insee.fr/fr/outil-interactif/7737357/legal-notice.html

The prepared index folds case, accents, apostrophes and repeated whitespace;
deduplicates names; removes aggregate suppression labels; and retains no sex,
birth counts or geographic information. These are project transformations,
not an INSEE publication or endorsement. Original source archives remain intact.

The preparation receipt records the exact editions, source URLs and SHA-256
digests. Attribution and provenance are also embedded in the SQLite metadata.
These files are public name statistics, not records identifying individuals,
not complete lists of names, and not annotated NER evaluation corpora.
